Everest · South Col route

The Mountain

5,364 m to 8,849 m on the real terrain, in the real weather of the day you choose. Acclimatise, read the forecast, turn around on time. Or don't.

Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM and other public DEMs). Weather: Open-Meteo (forecast, historical forecasts, ERA5).

How to climb Everest

The route

Base Camp → Khumbu Icefall → Camp 1 → Western Cwm → Camp 2 → Lhotse Face → Camp 3 → Yellow Band & Geneva Spur → South Col (Camp 4) → Balcony → South Summit → Hillary Step → Summit. Then you have to get down.

Acclimatise

Your body adapts to the height you spend time at. Go too high too fast and you will get AMS, then HAPE (fluid in the lungs) or HACE (swelling of the brain). Climb high, sleep low. Use the rotation option at Base Camp, or walk it.

Oxygen

Most climbers use bottled oxygen above Camp 3. [ ] change the flow. 2 L/min makes the summit feel like about 7,450 m. Watch the bottle.

Controls

W A S D walk, drag the mouse to look, scroll to zoom. Shift push harder (faster, more exhausting). E clip into fixed ropes, clip ladders, talk/help. Space self-arrest when you slip. R rest. C camp menu at a camp. 1 drink, 2 eat, 3 dexamethasone, 4 nifedipine. G goggles, L headlamp, Esc menu (call off the expedition), B show NPC behaviour trees, ` debug.

Time

Time is compressed while you walk, so a day on the mountain takes minutes. At camps you can rest, sleep, melt snow and wait out weather.

Weather

Past dates replay what the weather really did. The next two weeks use the live forecast. Summit winds above ~60 km/h are lethal. The leader will close the mountain.
